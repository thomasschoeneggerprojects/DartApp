DartTA is a fast, clean darts scoring app for your steel- or soft-tip board. Enter every dart with one tap, let the app do the maths and focus on your throw – fully offline, no account needed.

🎯 10 GAME MODES
• X01 (301 / 501 / 701 …) with straight, double or master in and out
• Cricket, Cut Throat Cricket and No Score Cricket with a clear marks board and MPR
• Around the Clock with a progress track
• Shanghai with round table and Shanghai meter
• Halve It, Bob's 27, Count Up and High Score

🤖 PLAY ALONE, WITH FRIENDS OR AGAINST THE BOT
• 1 to 8 players – mix humans and DartBots
• 20 bot levels, from beginner to strong club player
• Shuffle the throwing order, rematch with one tap

✅ MADE FOR THE OCHE
• Dart-by-dart entry with automatic turn change
• Checkout suggestions in X01
• Undo and redo for every dart
• Large buttons, dark design that is easy on the eyes

📈 TRAIN AND TRACK PROGRESS
• Target training with hit rate
• Daily goals
• Match history and statistics stored on your device

🌍 LANGUAGES
English, Deutsch and Español – follows your phone language or choose it in the settings.

🔒 PRIVACY
All games and statistics stay on your device. The app is free and shows a small banner ad; in the EU/UK you decide about personalised ads via the consent dialog and can change it at any time in Settings.

Good darts! 🎯
