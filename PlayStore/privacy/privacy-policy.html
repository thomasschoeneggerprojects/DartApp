DartTA – Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 November 2026

DartTA ("the app") is a darts scoring app published by Thomas Schönegger, Austria ("we"). This policy explains what data the app processes.

1. Data stored on your device

Player names, match results, training results, statistics and your language setting are stored only locally on your device. We do not run servers and never receive this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. Android's own backup may include it if you have enabled device backup.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). To deliver, measure and – if you agree – personalise ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID and app set ID,
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic / crash data of the SDK.

Data is transmitted encrypted. Details: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. Without consent, Google may still serve non-personalised or limited ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Austria: Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at). As we store no personal data on servers, requests regarding ad data are best directed to Google; we will gladly help.

6. Contact

Thomas Schönegger · E-mail: thomas.schoenegger@gmail.com

7. Changes

We will update this page when the app's data handling changes.

Gültig ab: 9. November 2026

Spielernamen, Spielergebnisse, Training, Statistiken und die Spracheinstellung werden ausschließlich lokal auf deinem Gerät gespeichert. Wir betreiben keine Server und erhalten diese Daten nie. Deinstallieren oder „Daten löschen“ entfernt sie.
